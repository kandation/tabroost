# Code of Conduct

We want Tabroost to be a friendly place to ask, report and build. This is a short version in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org, version 2.1).

## Our standard

Be kind and patient. Respect different opinions and experience levels. Give and accept feedback gracefully. Focus on what is best for users and the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other people's private information, sexual language or imagery, trolling, or spam.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues or ban people who break this code. Report problems privately to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Reports are handled in confidence.
